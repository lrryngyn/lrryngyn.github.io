About

Hi, my name is Larry Nguyen, an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Maryland (Class of 2027) with hands-on experience across circuit design, schematics, and PCB layout. I enjoy taking a circuit from initial calculations and simulation through PCB design and into physical hardware.

Recent work includes a 0–15V adjustable linear bench power supply, built from schematic and PCB layout through enclosure design, and a 12V-to-5V synchronous buck converter with PID control.

I've also gained hands-on electrical experience shadowing boiler and chiller operations at the Navy Yard and mechanical rooms at UMBC — working directly with contactors, relays, VFDs, and low-voltage systems.

Currently exploring Summer 2027 internship opportunities in circuit design, PCB layout, power electronics, and hardware development.
